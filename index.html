How it works

Capture, categorise, done

1

Write anything

Open Nevo and start typing. No folders to choose, no tags to assign — just put your thoughts down.

2

Nevo organises it

On-device AI reads your note and places it in the right category automatically. No data ever leaves your device.

3

Find it instantly

Browse by category or search across all your notes. Everything is exactly where you'd expect it to be.

11 Categories

Based on how people actually think

Nevo uses a deliberately small set of categories drawn from the PARA method — just enough structure to be useful, not so much that it becomes a system to maintain.

Everything stays on your device

Your notes are yours. Nevo processes everything locally using on-device AI — your thoughts are never sent to a server, never used for training, never seen by anyone but you.
