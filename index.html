Features

Intelligence that
respects your flow

Nevo uses a sophisticated multi-tier analysis system to understand your notes and categorize them instantly—without ever leaving your device.

🧠

On-Device AI

Powered by Apple Intelligence and CoreML, Nevo analyzes your notes locally. Your thoughts never leave your device—categorization happens in milliseconds, not on distant servers.

⚡

Instant Categorization

A three-tier system ensures accuracy: AI-powered analysis for deep understanding, ML classification for speed, and intelligent keyword matching as a reliable fallback.

📝

Rich Text Editing

Format your thoughts with bold, italic, headers, and more. The floating toolbar appears exactly when you need it, staying out of your way when you don't.

🔄

Adaptive Learning

When you recategorize a note, Nevo remembers. The system learns your preferences over time, becoming increasingly accurate to your personal organization style.
